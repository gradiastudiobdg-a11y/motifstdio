# Brand Context — Kai Makes Do

Updated: 2026-10-21. Status: adopted working identity for the studio by explicit user request on 2026-10-21. Handles and release cadence remain unverified/proposed.

Scope: the user's AI DIY creator account AND the local production studio. The user explicitly requested a full pivot from motivational MOTIF to Kai Makes Do. Preserve historical jobs as legacy content.

## Confirmed brief
- Silent AI-generated short videos for TikTok and YouTube Shorts. No dialogue or voiceover; tactile sound design is welcome.
- English identity and sparse English on-screen text for an international audience.
- A good-looking, approachable Asian male character, with a young-dad / friendly-neighbour feeling rather than an older uncle appearance.
- Simple home crafts that look plausible and achievable with ordinary materials and basic household tools. No professional woodworking or expert workshop image.
- A coherent header, title and footer system inspired by the supplied MOTIF screenshot, adapted to a lighter everyday-home identity.
- Eventual monetization is a business objective, not an assumed platform entitlement.

## Brand
- **Name**: Kai Makes Do (adopted working identity)
- **Category**: silent AI-crafted home DIY entertainment and visual inspiration
- **Description**: Kai turns everyday materials into small useful things for the home.
- **Stage**: pre-launch
- **Website / accounts**: not created; handles and domains not verified
- **Tagline**: Small ideas. Everyday stuff.
- **Short handle candidate**: @kaimakesdo, unverified

## Audience
- **Primary audience**: adults who enjoy small home upgrades, organizing, second-life materials, accessible crafts and satisfying process videos.
- **Working age hypothesis**: 22–40, all genders; not measured audience data.
- **Key problem**: DIY often looks too expensive, advanced or time-consuming to try.
- **Their language**: proposed copy directions include “I could try that”, “use what you have” and “a little more useful”; these are not research quotations.
- **Language approach**: simple English titles; actions carry the story. No country-specific slang or fake foreign identity.

## Positioning
- **Differentiation**: a consistent likeable virtual character, ordinary home, recognizable materials, small useful outcomes, tactile sound and legible steps.
- **Category alternatives**: expert woodworking channels, anonymous craft compilations, highly polished home styling. No named competitor benchmark has been completed.
- **Market position**: accessible and warm. An achievable idea with a tidy finish.
- **Promise**: “That looks small enough to try.” Avoid guaranteeing that a generated construction has been tested.

## Brand Personality
- **Personality Words**: warm, resourceful, curious, patient, lightly playful
- **Tone**: casual, calm, concise English; sparse text, no spoken lines
- **Visual reference**: supplied screenshot's label/title/footer hierarchy only; do not copy its motivational voice, heavy dark grade or red text shadow
- **Character**: Kai, fictional Asian man about 34; clean-shaven, short black hair, warm eyes, natural attractive appearance, average lean build, plain sage cotton tee, no apron
- **Setting**: ordinary dining table or porch, daylight, cream wall, a plant, basic tools

## Values & Mission
- **Core Values**: usefulness, accessibility, resourcefulness, clarity, honesty about AI
- **Mission**: Make everyday home creativity feel approachable through small, satisfying visual stories.
- **Transparency**: disclose photorealistic AI content using each platform's applicable upload setting; explain the fictional AI character in the bio/about text. Concepts are not proof of real builds or product testing.

## Goals
- **Primary Goal**: develop a recognizable creator brand and test a sustainable content format, then explore suitable monetization.
- **Pilot**: 12 original episodes in four weeks, three per week; a working proposal, not a user commitment.
- **Key Metrics**: completion and average percentage viewed, saves per view, shares per view, follows per 1,000 views, production time/cost per accepted episode.
- **Monetization order**: qualify and validate audience first; consider transparently presented materials/affiliate links, brand partnerships and genuinely verified digital guides later. Platform ad/rewards eligibility must be checked independently.
